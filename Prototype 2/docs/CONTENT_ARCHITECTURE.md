# Content Architecture

Working outline only. Confirm content and priorities before fixing page structure.

## Candidate content areas

- Identity and positioning
- Selected projects with contribution, evidence, status, and links
- About / background
- Capabilities supported by real examples
- Experience or learning milestones, accurately labeled
- Contact and approved public profiles

A single-page starting point may suit the material; decide after the inventory and creative exploration. Dedicated project pages should be added only when verified material supports them. Navigation, order, labels, and storytelling are open.

## Content rules

See `CONTENT_INVENTORY.md`. Keep claims tied to evidence, distinguish planned/in-progress/completed work, and omit unverified details.
